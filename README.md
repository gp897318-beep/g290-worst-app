A make.fun entry for [The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23): one sentence pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable — judged pairwise by a deadpan product-critic judge until the most brilliantly pointless idea takes the $2.

`main.ts` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` after validating it against the gate (1–240 characters, not only whitespace):

> FridgePeek is a $4.99-a-month app that texts busy professionals a photo of their own fridge every time they open it, so they can confirm from the office, in real time, that the milk they already know is in there is still in there.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```
